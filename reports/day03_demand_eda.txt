12. What this tells us
----------------------
- Demand is geographically uneven: some wards generate substantially more searches and bookings than others.
- Searches, bookings, and completed trips should be treated as related but distinct demand/activity measures.
- Conversion rate adds a quality/efficiency dimension; high activity does not automatically mean high conversion.
- The top-10 concentration figures quantify how much activity is concentrated in the busiest wards.
- The results support ward-level demand and operational analysis in Power BI.

13. What we cannot conclude
-----------------------------
- We cannot identify peak hours, weekdays, months, or seasonality because there is no time field.
- We cannot analyse individual routes or pickup/drop locations because this is ward-level aggregate data.
- We cannot claim that a ward causes higher demand based only on these correlations.
- We should not invent demographic, traffic, weather, income, or service-level explanations without additional data.
