Derived features
-----------------
- Completion_Rate_From_Bookings = Completed Trips / Bookings × 100
- Fare_Per_Km = Average Fare / Average Distance per Trip
- Earnings_Per_Completed_Trip = Drivers Earnings / Completed Trips
- Distance_Per_Completed_Trip_Km = Distance Travelled / Completed Trips
- Booking_to_Search_Rate = Bookings / Searches × 100
- Percentile features for major volume/performance metrics
- Quartile segments for demand, conversion and cancellation rate

Interpretation rules
--------------------
- Descriptive statistics summarize the 244 observed wards; they do not establish causation.
- Quartiles are relative to this dataset and are descriptive, not ML clusters.
- IQR flags are screening indicators, not proof of bad data.
- Fare-per-km is a derived comparison metric, not an official pricing formula.
- Earnings-per-trip is derived from aggregate fields and is not an audited payout figure.
- No temporal statistics or forecasting are possible because the dataset has no date/time field.
