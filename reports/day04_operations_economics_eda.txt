Interpretation notes
--------------------
1. Cancellation rate shows the share of bookings that were cancelled; the dataset does not explain why cancellations occurred.
2. Fare and distance are examined as an association, not as proof that distance causes fare.
3. Earnings and completed trips are compared to understand scale/economic relationships, not to claim causation.
4. Estimated fare revenue and earnings-per-trip are analytical calculations from aggregate fields, not audited company financial figures.
5. No hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly operational trends can be measured because the dataset has no date/time field.
